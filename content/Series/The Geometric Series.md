
# Geometric series
: multiply each term by a fixed number $r$

$$
a + ar + ar^2 + \cdots + ar^{n-1} + \cdots
$$

---
## The sum of $n$ terms of the geometric progression


$$
S_n = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r}
$$

---
## The sum of the geometric series

$$
S = \lim_{n\to\infty} S_n = \frac{a}{1-r}, \quad \mathrm{if\ \  |r|<1 \ \ (convergent). }
$$
