

## Absolute series

>[!note] Absolute series
>A series $\to$ (make every term positive) $\to$ Absolute series


### Absolutely convergent

>[!note] Absolutely convergent
>If the absolute series converges, then it is called absolutely convergent.
>If the absolute series is convergent, then the series also converges.
