
## Preliminary test


> [!note] Preliminary Test
> If the terms of an infinite series do *not* tend to zero (that is, if $\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n \neq 0$), the series diverges. 
> 
> If $\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n = 0$), we must test further.

This preliminary test can *never* tell you that a series converges.

>[!caution]  The harmonic series 
> $\sum^{\infty}_{n=1} 1/n$ is divergent even though $\lim_{n\to\infty} 1/n = 0$.
