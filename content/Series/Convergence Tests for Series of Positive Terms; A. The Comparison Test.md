

>[!note] Comparison test (1)
>Let two series $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$.
>If (1) $\sum b_n$ converges and (2) $|a_n| \leq b_n$ for all $n$ from some point ont,
>then $\sum a_n$ is absolutely convergent.

>[!note] Comparison test (2)
>Let two series $\sum a_n$ and $\sum d_n$.
>If (1) $\sum d_n$ has only positive terms and diverges and (2) $|a_n| \geq d_n$ for all $n$ from some point,
>then $\sum a_n$ is divergent.
